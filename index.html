My first 30 days as a BDE

A new hire who arrives with a plan ramps up faster. This is how I would spend the first month.

Week 1: Learn

Study the product, the pricing and the best customers. Sit in on calls, read past deals and learn why customers said yes or no.

Week 2: Prepare

Build a target list of 50 companies, write email and call scripts, and set up a clean CRM routine.

Weeks 3-4: Reach out

Start daily calls, emails and LinkedIn messages. Log every touchpoint and follow up at least five times.

Day 30: Review

Share my numbers with my manager: calls, meetings booked and what worked. Then adjust the plan.
